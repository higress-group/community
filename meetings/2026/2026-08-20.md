## Meeting minutes and conclusions

The source document is primarily the meeting outline and includes only limited
annotations from the discussion; it is not a full transcript. The minutes

### Community updates

- The public community meeting resumed with an initial monthly cadence. The
  frequency may be adjusted if the number of topics grows.
- Higress v2.2.4 was released with updates for MCP, Gateway API Inference
  Extension, AI inference scheduling, observability, and bug fixes.
- CNCF incubation preparation is in progress. The community is looking for
  five to seven adopter organizations that are willing to participate in CNCF
  interviews.
- Security reports should be submitted to both the
  [GitHub Private Security Advisory](https://github.com/higress-group/higress/security/advisories/new)
  and the [Alibaba Security Response Center](https://security.alibaba.com/).
- Large features and design changes should begin with a Proposal so that the
  community can discuss the scope before implementation.

### Proposal follow-up recorded

- The source outline identified Gateway API Inference Extension v1.5, llm-d,
  AAIF, and the OpenTelemetry GenAI semantic conventions as relevant external
  work for Higress.
- The limited meeting annotations retained follow-up questions for Agent,
  Sandbox, and A2A support, including permissions, external Sandbox
  integration, coordination with Sandbox products, common OpenSandbox usage,
  changes to Ingress resources, and the effect on the user workflow.
- The unified AI control plane and the cache, session, and structured-output
  proposals will continue in their GitHub issues after the meeting.
- The source notes do not record final prioritization decisions for the other
  proposals on the agenda.

### Meeting conclusion

The meeting re-established a regular public community sync and a
Proposal-first process for significant changes. It also identified inference
scheduling, MCP compatibility and security, AI observability, Agent and
Sandbox integration, A2A, and the AI control plane as continuing areas of
investigation. Most technical topics remained in their linked GitHub issues;
the source record does not establish a complete next-release priority or a
named owner for every follow-up.

## Decisions

- Continue the public community meeting on a monthly cadence and adjust the
  frequency if needed.
- Discuss large features and design changes through Proposals before starting
  implementation.
