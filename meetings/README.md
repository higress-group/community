# Higress Community Meeting Records

This directory stores durable public records for Higress community meetings.
The schedule, joining information, and agenda process are documented in
[`MEETINGS.md`](../MEETINGS.md).

Meeting records are organized as `YYYY/YYYY-MM-DD.md` and contain:

- meeting date, time, calendar entry, and joining link;
- agenda topics, proposers, and related public issues or pull requests;
- discussion notes and decisions;
- action items with owners and status; and
- recording and transcript links after processing.

Use [`meeting-notes-template.md`](./meeting-notes-template.md) when adding a
record. Significant technical or governance decisions must also be recorded in
the relevant public issue or pull request.
